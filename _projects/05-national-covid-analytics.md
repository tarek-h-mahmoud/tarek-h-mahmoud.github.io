### Problem

Government decision-makers needed real-time epidemiological forecasts and risk assessments to guide policy during the pandemic, in a form interpretable by non-technical stakeholders.

### My role

Principal data scientist leading development of the forecasting, estimation, simulation, and risk-scoring models.

### Approach

Several linked components: incidence forecasting via exponential smoothing and count regression models; R₀/Rₑ estimation; city-scale multi-agent epidemic simulation for spread scenarios; contact tracing; and individual risk scoring using logistic regression and deep learning, extended to geographic and occupational risk profiling and vaccine prioritization.

### Outcome

Directly informed national policy decisions. The work was recognized by name in the government's official COVID-19 response publication.

### Technologies

Exponential smoothing, Poisson and zero-inflated negative binomial regression, agent-based simulation, logistic regression, deep learning, Python.
