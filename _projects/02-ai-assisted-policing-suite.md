### Problem

Law-enforcement agencies needed integrated AI capabilities to streamline investigations, connect evidence across cases, and prioritize leads at scale.

### My role

Principal data scientist and technical lead for core analytical capabilities contributing to the suite, working alongside the wider product team rather than owning the product in full.

### Approach

Capabilities span suspect profiling, cross-case correlation, timeline and narrative reconstruction, hypothesis analysis, evidence intelligence, and legal analysis. These combine NLP over case notes, graph analytics across entity and relationship networks, and machine-learning models for behavioural pattern detection and risk prioritization.

### Outcome

Deployed into operational use across multiple agencies, supporting investigative workflows on live casework.

### Technologies

Natural language processing, graph analytics, machine-learning models, Python, case-management system integration.
