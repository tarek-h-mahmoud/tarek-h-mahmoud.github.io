### Problem

A public-sector client needed to fuse dozens of disparate structured and unstructured sources into coherent, actionable behavioural signals, where no single source was sufficient on its own.

### My role

Architect of the system design and lead on implementation, combining symbolic logic, statistical models, and graph-derived signals.

### Approach

A big-data ingestion and fusion layer draws on more than 50 heterogeneous sources, feeding rule-based expert-system logic alongside supervised machine-learning models. Knowledge-graph structure contributes relational features that individual records do not expose.

### Outcome

Became the flagship product for the organization's public-sector vertical and a large recurring engagement.

### Technologies

Big-data pipelines, knowledge graphs, expert-system logic, supervised machine learning, graph analytics, Python, PySpark.
